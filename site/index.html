How you decide

  1. 1One link is enough

    Paste a shop link: Propario takes the picture, price and dimensions straight from the shop – or bring your table from Excel or your AI.

  2. 2Define your requirements

    Set what it must be, what you wish for and a budget: every product is checked against them – green met, red not met.

  3. 3Side by side, not all over the place

    The candidates side by side: best values shown, differences highlighted – and their sizes to scale, in 3D too.

  4. 4Sort out quickly

    Swipe: thumb up or down, each person on their own. What nobody likes is sorted out – without a long discussion.

  5. 5Settle it in duels

    Which is better? Propario puts them side by side and asks until your ranking stands. On a phone, AR puts them right into your room.

  6. 6Decide together

    Your rankings become one joint ranking – its first place can become your decision. Then the purchase, returns and warranty in view.

With your AI, if you like.

“Copy for my AI” hands the comparison to the assistant you already use. Its answer comes back as a table and updates your products – you see every change first.

Private, safe, yours.

  • Runs on your own server – your data stays with you
  • Passkeys instead of passwords
  • Stored encrypted, shared only on purpose
  • No tracking, no advertising, no affiliate links
  • Accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA), free software (AGPL)

New people join only by invitation. A shop page is read once when you add it, never crawled, and shops that refuse automated access are respected. This page has no cookies, no tracking and no scripts either.

Get started

Propario runs in Docker. On your own computer, this is all it takes:

git clone https://github.com/schlatter/propario.git && cd propario
echo "DATA_KEY=$(openssl rand -base64 32)" >> .env
docker compose up -d
docker compose logs app    # shows the one-time setup link

Open the setup link, choose a name, create your passkey – and invite the people you decide with. Or press Try the demo on the start page: invented comparisons that never leave your browser.

Questions

Is there a hosted version I can sign up for?

No. Propario is meant to run on your own server, for a household or a group of friends. If someone you know runs an instance, ask them for an invitation.

What do I need to run it?

Docker on a small server or a computer at home. To use it beyond localhost, a hostname with HTTPS – passkeys require it. It works well behind a VPN, without any public exposure.

Which shops does it work with?

Any shop page that states its product's picture, name or price in a common format. For shops that refuse automated access, Propario takes what the link itself says and you add the rest.

Can I bring my existing spreadsheets?

Yes. Excel, CSV and Markdown tables are imported with a preview of every column; unknown columns become fields, nothing is dropped. You can export to Excel and JSON at any time.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Propario is free software under the AGPL-3.0. If you run a modified version for others, they are entitled to its source.
