Compare products together, and decide.

The sideboard, the laptop, the bike: Propario keeps the requirements, the candidates, their pictures and everyone's opinion in one calm comparison – and checks every product against what you need. On your own server.

How you decide

  1. One link is enough

    Paste a shop link: picture, price and dimensions come straight from the page. Or bring your table from Excel or your AI.

  2. Say what you need

    Musts, wishes and a budget. Every product is checked: green met, red not met, grey not known yet.

  3. See them side by side

    Differences highlighted, sizes to scale against the space you have – in 3D, and on a phone in your room.

  4. Decide together

    Everyone swipes and duels on their own; the rankings become one. Then the purchase, returns and warranty in view.

Your requirements check every product

Write down what the purchase has to do – fits the niche, at most 180 cm wide – and Propario checks each candidate. Musts decide who is eligible; the rest becomes a weighted fit. Missing data never counts against a product: it stays grey until someone knows.

Side by side, to scale

Pick your candidates and look at them next to each other: best values marked, only the differences if you like, and their sizes drawn to scale next to a person – with the dashed line of the space your requirements leave.

Made for deciding together

Each person swipes through the products – like, neutral, dislike – and settles the favourites in duels. No long discussion about what nobody likes, and a joint ranking whose first place can become your decision.

And everything around it

  • Your AI, if you like. “Copy for my AI” hands the comparison to the assistant you use; its answer comes back as a table – you see every change first.
  • Import what you have. Excel, CSV or Markdown, with a preview and a mapping you can correct. Nothing is dropped silently.
  • Offers, tests and reviews. Several shops per product, ratings on one scale, price comparisons by GTIN.
  • A table like a spreadsheet. Edit in place, sort, filter with chips or a query, choose and move columns.
  • An app on your phone. Install it, and share a product from a shop app straight into a comparison.
  • For everyone. English and German, light and dark, built and tested for WCAG 2.1 AA.

Private, safe, yours

  • Runs on your own server – your data stays with you, encrypted at rest.
  • Passkeys instead of passwords; new people join only by invitation.
  • Every comparison is private until you share it – to view or to edit.
  • No tracking, no advertising, no affiliate links, no third-party scripts. This page has none either.
  • A shop page is read once, never crawled; shops that refuse are respected.
  • Free software under the AGPL – read it, change it, run it.

Get started

Propario runs in Docker. On your own computer, this is all it takes:

git clone https://github.com/schlatter/propario.git && cd propario
echo "DATA_KEY=$(openssl rand -base64 32)" >> .env
docker compose up -d
docker compose logs app    # shows the one-time setup link

Open the setup link, choose a name, create your passkey – and invite the people you decide with. Or press Try the demo on the start page: invented comparisons that never leave your browser.

Questions

Is there a hosted version I can sign up for?

No. Propario is meant to run on your own server, for a household or a group of friends. If someone you know runs an instance, ask them for an invitation.

What do I need to run it?

Docker on a small server or a computer at home. To use it beyond localhost, a hostname with HTTPS – passkeys require it. It works well behind a VPN, without any public exposure.

Which shops does it work with?

Any shop page that states its product's picture, name or price in a common format. Propario reads a page once when you add it and never gets past a bot wall; for shops that refuse, it takes what the link itself says and you add the rest.

Can I bring my existing spreadsheets?

Yes. Excel, CSV and Markdown tables are imported with a preview of every column; unknown columns become fields, nothing is dropped. You can also export to Excel and JSON at any time.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Propario is free software under the AGPL-3.0. If you run a modified version for others, they are entitled to its source.
